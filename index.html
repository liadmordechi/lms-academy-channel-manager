LMS Academy Channel Manager

An internal tool that LMS ACADEMY LLC uses to publish and organize its own soccer training videos on its own YouTube channel. It is not a product, it is not distributed, and it has one operator.

Read the Privacy Policy

What it is

LMS ACADEMY LLC is a youth soccer training academy operating two locations in Boca Raton and Hollywood, Florida. We produce our own video content — recorded training sessions, drill demonstrations, technique breakdowns and match highlights — and publish it to our own YouTube channel, @lmsocceracademy.

This tool completes the publishing steps for that content. It runs locally on one computer, is operated by one person — the channel owner — and is not available to anyone else.

What it does

  • Uploads finished video files to our own channel
  • Applies custom thumbnails
  • Writes structured titles, descriptions and tags using our own conventions
  • Attaches caption tracks so our content is accessible
  • Files each video into the correct playlist by program and age group
  • Reports upload and processing status for our own videos

Scope of access

The tool authenticates through Google OAuth 2.0 as the channel owner and requests three scopes:

  • youtube.upload
  • youtube
  • youtube.force-ssl

It reads and writes only our own channel. It does not access, collect, or store data belonging to any other user or channel. It does not aggregate data across channels, does not use search.list, and does not expose YouTube data to any third party. There is no server, no database, and no public interface. Full detail is in the Privacy Policy.
